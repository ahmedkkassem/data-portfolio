Monday Report

One dashboard for a whole online store, built on the real transaction log of a UK online gift retailer: 1.07 million invoice lines from December 2009 to December 2011. Every number comes out of a tested dbt pipeline, and an independent script re-checks it to the penny before the page can publish.
