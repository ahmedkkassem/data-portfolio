Spreadsheet rescue

Three sales teams keep their monthly files three different ways. The pipeline reads all three, repairs what is wrong and proves, line by line, that nothing was lost, changed or counted twice.

How it's built

Runs inside the same daily pipeline

StepToolWhat it does
ReadPython (openpyxl, csv)Finds the real header row, maps each team's column names onto one set, and parses dates and numbers by each team's format
RepairGeneric rulesDrops title, subtotal and blank rows; removes pasted-twice rows; fixes codes, customers, countries and return signs
Provedbt testCompares every rebuilt line with the source, field by field and in both directions; revenue must match to the penny

The three files are generated from the real November 2011 lines of the Monday Report data, with common spreadsheet problems planted on purpose, so there is a known right answer to check against. With a client's real files, the same checks compare against their accounting or order totals.
